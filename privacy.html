Privacy policy: Personal OS

This site covers two personal apps. Each is used only by its owner, Marvin Ang, with his own Google accounts. Neither app is offered to anyone else.

Personal OS Backup only accesses files it creates in the owner's own Google Drive, to store encrypted backups.

Personal OS Connect has read-only access to the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Sheets. It copies his email, calendar events and chosen sheets to his own private server, so that he can search and organise them and be alerted to urgent items. It cannot send email, change or delete anything, or mark email as read. It never writes to Google.

What happens to the data.

  • The data is kept on the owner's private server and in his own encrypted backups.
  • Parts of it may be sent to an AI service chosen by the owner, only to label and summarise items for him.
  • It isn't sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Personal OS Connect's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
